ERROR LOGGER  -  quick start
============================

What it does: after a mock test, one click grabs every question you got wrong or
skipped, an AI sorts them by subject / topic / type of mistake, and they become
flashcards on a spaced-repetition schedule. All on your own PC.
Works on Testbook, Physics Wallah, Oliveboard and TestRanking.


INSTALL  (5-15 minutes, mostly downloading)
-------------------------------------------
1. Unzip this whole folder first - don't run anything from inside the zip.
2. Double-click  Install.bat
3. Blue "Windows protected your PC" box?  More info -> Run anyway.
4. Two questions come up. Pressing Enter picks the right option both times.
5. At the end Chrome opens its extensions page. Do these four things:
      - turn ON  "Developer mode"   (top-right toggle)
      - click    "Load unpacked"
      - press Ctrl+V (the folder path is already on your clipboard), Enter
      - click    "Select Folder", then pin the extension (puzzle icon)

No admin rights needed. Nothing is installed system-wide.


DAILY USE
---------
1. Leave the small black console window open. If it is closed, use the
   "Error Logger" shortcut on your Desktop.
2. Finish your mock, open the solution / analysis page.
3. Click the floating  "Log Mistakes"  button (bottom right)
   -> "Scan page for wrong answers", and wait a few seconds per question.
4. Open  http://localhost:8787/  -> Review tab -> do your flashcards.


SOMETHING BROKEN?
-----------------
Re-run Install.bat. It is safe to run any number of times and fixes most things.

Full manual - models, switching to offline mode, backing up your flashcards,
every error message explained:  see  README.md  in this folder.

To remove everything:  Uninstall.bat  (it asks before deleting your flashcards).
